Example

1/2 + 1/3 = ?

1. The denominators are different.
We cannot add halves and thirds directly.
2. Find a common denominator.
The smallest number both 2 and 3 divide into is 6.
3. Rename both fractions.
1/2 = 3/6 and 1/3 = 2/6.
4. Add the numerators.
3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6. Keep the denominator 6.
Remember: same denominator first, then add or subtract the top numbers.
