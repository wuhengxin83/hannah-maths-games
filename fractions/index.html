1/2 + 1/3 = ?
1. Look at the bottom numbers.
2 and 3 are different, so the pieces are different sizes.
2. Make the pieces the same size.
The first number that 2 and 3 can both make is 6.
3. Rename the fractions.
1/2 becomes 3/6. 1/3 becomes 2/6.
4. Now add the top numbers.
3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.
